# Statlib

[Statlib](https://github.com/stat-lib/statlib) is an open community that aims to support the verification of classical, contemporary, and emerging research in mathematical statistics; its vision and goals are shaped by the whole community. Please check out our contributors [below](#activity). We build with [Institute for Computer-Aided Reasoning in Mathematics](https://icarm.io/) at CMU.

Interested in joining us? Visit our [Zulip channel](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/611809-Statlib).
